LOCAL CREATOR STUDIO

Ideas become short videos.

Interledge Studio helps its creator turn educational ideas into short-form videos in Turkish and English, then review each result before sharing.

Creator controlled Runs locally Review before posting

TIKTOK INTEGRATION

The creator stays in control.

01

Connect

TikTok Login Kit securely connects the creator's own account with explicit permission.

02

Select

The creator chooses a completed MP4 video and confirms that it may be transferred.

03

Private post & review

After explicit confirmation, the video is posted with Only You visibility. The creator reviews it in TikTok before changing its visibility.

WHAT THE APP DOES

Short-form knowledge, prepared locally.

Interledge Studio combines creator-provided topics, licensed stock footage, narration, captions and local video editing. It never posts without explicit creator confirmation, and TikTok posts are initially visible only to the creator.
