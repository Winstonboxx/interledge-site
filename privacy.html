Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Interledge Studio handles information when a creator uses the local desktop application and its optional platform integrations.

1. Local-first operation

Interledge Studio runs on the creator's own computer. Project files, generated videos, settings and platform authorization tokens are stored locally unless the creator chooses to send content to a third-party service.

2. Information processed

When the creator connects TikTok, the application may receive basic account information permitted by the user.info.basic scope, such as display name, profile image and an app-specific account identifier. OAuth access and refresh tokens are kept locally so the connection can operate.

When the creator explicitly approves an upload, the selected video file is transferred from the creator's computer to TikTok for placement in the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.

3. Third-party services

Features chosen by the creator may communicate directly with TikTok, Google Gemini, YouTube, Pexels or Pixabay. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Interledge Studio does not sell personal information.

4. Website data

This informational website does not provide user accounts, advertising, analytics, contact forms or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process basic request information, such as IP addresses, for security and delivery purposes.

5. Retention and control

Locally stored projects and credentials remain on the creator's computer until the creator removes them. The creator can revoke TikTok access through TikTok account settings and remove local authorization data by disconnecting or deleting the application's local token files.

6. Security

Client secrets and authorization tokens are not intentionally exposed through this website. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

Interledge Studio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application or its integrations change. Questions can be submitted through the Winstonboxx GitHub profile.
